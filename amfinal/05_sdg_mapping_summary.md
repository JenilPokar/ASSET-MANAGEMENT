# SDG Mapping Summary

**Project:** Asset Management System (Multi-Tenant SaaS Edition)
**Developed by:** Jenil Pokar | Roll No. 46 | FY AI-DS

This project maps to three Sustainable Development Goals through concrete,
working features rather than generic aspiration:

## SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
The platform is itself a piece of digital infrastructure: a single
multi-tenant system lets schools, hospitals, and industrial units each run
modern, database-backed asset tracking without building or hosting their own
software. The MySQL-driven business logic (stored procedures, functions,
triggers) replaces manual registers and spreadsheets with automated,
auditable industrial-grade record-keeping — directly supporting resilient,
technology-enabled infrastructure for organizations that might otherwise have
none.

## SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production
The Asset_Maintenance and Asset_Disposal modules formalize a full
asset lifecycle rather than a "buy and forget" model. Maintenance scheduling
extends usable life before replacement; the Disposal module requires every
retired asset to be logged with a method (Sold, Scrapped, Donated, Recycled,
Lost), making disposal outcomes visible and reportable instead of untracked.
The depreciation function also gives each organization a real, ongoing sense
of an asset's remaining value — encouraging reuse/repair decisions over
premature replacement.

## SDG 13 — Climate Action
By centralizing maintenance schedules and warranty tracking (with automatic
alerts 30 days before expiry), the system reduces unnecessary early
replacement of functioning equipment — one of the more effective everyday
levers organizations have for cutting the embodied-carbon footprint of
electronics and machinery. The Disposal module's "Recycled" and "Donated"
tracking also gives each tenant company visibility into how much of their
retired hardware is being diverted from landfill versus discarded.
